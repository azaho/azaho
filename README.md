## About Me 👋

I am a Member of Technical Staff at [Merge Labs](https://merge.io/), working at the intersection of neuroscience and machine learning.

I completed my undergraduate and master's studies at [MIT](https://www.mit.edu/) in Computer Science and Brain and Cognitive Sciences, where my research focused on representation learning from large-scale human neural recordings. At MIT, I was advised by [Ila Fiete](https://fietelab.mit.edu/) and previously worked with Dr. [Guangyu Robert Yang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robert-yang-41a83019) and Dr. [Christopher Cueva](https://www.metaconscious.org/author/chris-cueva/).

I co-founded the [Ukraine Leadership and Technology Academy](https://ultacademy.org/) to equip brilliant young Ukrainians with the skills, knowledge, and confidence they need to rebuild Ukraine into a thriving, European nation. I also won the [Schwarzman Scholarship](https://news.mit.edu/2025/mit-students-named-schwarzman-scholars-0115) (class of 2026), and am a [Cerebras Fellow](https://cerebras.ai/blog/aibi-revolutionizing-ai-interviewing). I co-organize [TEDxMIT](https://tedx.mit.edu/team). In 2025, I received the Glushko Prize for Outstanding Research; in 2026, I was elected to Full Membership in Sigma Xi, The Scientific Research Honor Society.
